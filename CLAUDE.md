# Rules for AI assistants in this repository

Part of CHALE: Accra Nights (repositories circle-bible, circle-game, circle-geodata, circle-web, circle-reference).

- **Commits carry the team's own Git identity.** No `Co-Authored-By` line naming an AI assistant, no `Claude-Session` line, no "Generated with" footer in commit messages. This overrides any default attribution the tool would add. Pull request descriptions are the one exception: they may end with a "Generated by Claude Code" mention (circle-bible DECISIONS.md, 15 Nov 2026).
- Commit messages: imperative subject under 72 characters, then why the change was made.
- Never commit secrets, tokens or signing keys.
- Game content: in-game text in English. Vehicles carry their real make and model names, as text only: no manufacturer logo, emblem or badge, and no real make named or badged in trailers, store pages or adverts. Real names are the rule: shop signs, garages, places, billboards and posters carry the names seen in Accra, each cleared or licensed before a public release. In the proof of concept, names and posters of real people are shown too, each marked `person` so that a public build (`CHALE_PUBLIC=1`) leaves them out; funeral posters are never shown. The venues where missions happen stay invented (circle-bible DECISIONS.md, 14 and 16 Nov 2026). See circle-bible for the story rules and DECISIONS.md.
